# Code of conduct

FormaTranslate welcomes people of every background and level of experience. We
want contributors and users to be able to ask questions, report problems and
disagree about technical decisions without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect. Critique an idea or implementation, not the person.
- Give useful context, listen to feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Be patient with different languages, abilities and experience levels.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic examples when discussing translation problems;
  do not publish someone else's documents, personal information or credentials.
- Keep discussions relevant and follow a maintainer's request to de-escalate.

Harassment, discriminatory or degrading language, threats, sexual attention or
content directed at others, sustained personal attacks, impersonation and the
publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other
community spaces, and when someone represents FormaTranslate in public. It
applies to maintainers as well as other participants.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the project maintainer, [@grebocheck](https://github.com/grebocheck). To
arrange a private reporting channel, open a minimal
[issue](https://github.com/grebocheck/FormaTranslate/issues/new) titled
"Private community report contact". Include only a request for private contact;
do not include the incident, names, documents, screenshots or personal contact
details in that public issue. The maintainer will arrange a channel before
requesting the details. A GitHub issue itself is public, not a confidential report.

In the private report, describe the behavior, where it happened and any relevant
links or evidence you are comfortable sharing. Reports will be handled with
respect for the reporter's privacy, with details shared only as needed to assess
and address the concern. For software vulnerabilities, follow the separate
[security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates this code and may
restrict participation. They will consider the impact, severity and pattern of
behavior. Responses may include a request to correct the behavior, a private
warning, a temporary restriction or a permanent ban. Serious threats or
harassment can warrant immediate restriction without prior warning.

When practical, the affected participant will receive an explanation and an
opportunity to provide relevant context. A participant may request a review of a
decision through the same reporting process. A maintainer involved in an incident
should seek an uninvolved reviewer where one is available and disclose when the
project's small maintainer team makes that impossible.
